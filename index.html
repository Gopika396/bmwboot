What is BMW?

BMW (Bayerische Motoren Werke) is a German automobile manufacturer known for luxury vehicles and high-performance engineering.

The Ultimate Driving Machine

BMW's famous slogan reflects its focus on performance, precision engineering and an unmatched driving experience.

When Was BMW Founded?

BMW was founded in 1916 in Munich, Germany, originally as an aircraft engine manufacturer before moving into automobiles.
